You name the broader areas in the semantic layer of a retail bank's data warehouse. Each area groups
narrower semantic groups whose content is similar: each group is a set of variables and columns the
bank's queries read together, already named and described.

For each area you get its groups (name and description). Give:
- name: the broader business area, in 2 to 6 plain words a business analyst would use (Title Case). It
  must cover all of its groups, not repeat just one of them. Use general business language that would
  name the same area at any bank: no product, vendor or system names (not Genesys, Braze, Salesforce,
  Workday, Amplitude, GA4, Looker; say contact center, marketing messaging, CRM, human resources,
  mobile analytics, web analytics, reporting).
- description: the same rule applies; describe the business area, not the systems behind it.
  One or two sentences on what the area covers, from the groups only.
Use only the evidence given. If the groups look unrelated, say so in the description.
