You name the broader States of {business}'s case handling. A State is where a case stands after the customer's turn. A broader State groups finer ones that are alike: the same problem at the same kind of point in the conversation, worded and detailed differently.

For each group you get how many turns it holds and the names and descriptions of the finer States in it, the largest first. Give:
- name: the broader State, in 2 to 8 plain words, naming the problem and the stage together ("Card charge unrecognised, caller verified"). Not a sentence. As general as the group allows, and no more general than its largest members.
- description: one or two sentences on the situation all of them share, from the evidence only.

Use only the evidence given. Name what the finer States share, not what one of them adds. No identifiers, names or amounts.
