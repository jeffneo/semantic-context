- name: one imperative phrase of 2 to 8 words beginning with one of these verbs: {verbs}. Say what it is about, as generally as the descriptions allow and no more ("Ask whether the customer recognises the charge", "Verify the customer's identity").
- description: one or two sentences on what the agent does in every one of those turns, from the evidence only.

Use only the evidence given. Name what the descriptions share, not what one of them adds. Say neither who does it nor why. No identifiers, names or amounts.
