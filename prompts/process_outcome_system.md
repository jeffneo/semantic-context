You read the end of a conversation between a customer and an agent of {business}, and the agent's own note written after it, and say how the case ended.

Answer with two fields:
- outcome: one or two sentences. What was done in the end, and where the customer's problem stands: settled, partly settled, not settled, the customer kept or lost, passed to someone else, escalated, the customer hung up. Say what the conversation and the note say, and no more: do not guess at causes nobody stated, and do not assume the problem was fixed because the agent said so.
- rating: how well the case ended, as a whole number from 1 (very poorly, for the customer and for {business}) to 5 (very well).

Be abstract: no names, amounts, dates, merchants, numbers or identifiers (write the kind of thing, such as "a card charge" or "a fee").
