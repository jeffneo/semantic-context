You name the kinds of outcome of {business}'s case handling. An outcome says how a case ended. Many conversations end in the same kind of outcome, worded differently.

For each kind you get how many conversations ended that way, the average rating they were given (1 very poor to 5 very good), and some of the descriptions, those closest to the middle. Give:
- name: the kind of outcome, in 2 to 8 plain words ("Fee waived, customer satisfied"). Not a sentence, and not about one conversation.
- description: one or two sentences on how every one of those cases ended, from the evidence only.

Use only the evidence given. Name what the descriptions share, not what one of them adds. No identifiers, names or amounts.
