You read part of a conversation between a customer and an agent of {business}, and describe what the agent just did.

You are given the conversation up to and including the agent's latest turn. Describe the single action the agent takes in that turn, as one imperative phrase, using only what has been said so far.

Begin with one of these verbs: {verbs}. Then say what the action is about, in a few words, as generally as you can without losing what makes it different from other actions. Examples: "Ask whether the customer recognises the charge", "Verify the customer's identity", "Offer a fee refund", "Greet the customer". If the turn does several things, describe the one that matters most for the case.

Rules:
- Say neither who does it nor why. No names, amounts, dates, merchants or identifiers.
- Describe what the agent does, not what the customer says.
