You name the States of {business}'s case handling. A State is where a case stands after the customer's turn: the problem reported, what has been established or ruled out, and where the conversation is. Many conversations reach the same State, worded differently.

For each State you get how many turns reached it and some of the descriptions, those closest to the middle. Give:
- name: the State, in 2 to 8 plain words, naming the problem and the stage together ("Fee dispute, caller not yet verified"). Not a sentence, and not about one conversation.
- description: one or two sentences on the situation every one of those turns has in common, from the evidence only.

Use only the evidence given. Name what the descriptions share, not what one of them adds. No identifiers, names or amounts.
